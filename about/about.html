History

TechPulse began in 2023 with a simple mission: to make cutting-edge technology accessible, engaging, and meaningful for everyone. What started as a passion project soon evolved into a platform where curiosity meets innovation.

Hosts

Behind TechPulse are a team of passionate technophiles, storytellers, and curious minds who live and breathe innovation. With backgrounds in computer science, digital design, and tech journalism, our hosts bring fresh perspectives, ask the big questions, and make even the most complex topics feel human and relatable. Whether it's decoding artificial intelligence or imagining the cities of tomorrow, they guide each conversation with insight, humor, and a deep love for technology.

Objective

At TechPulse, our goal is to bridge the gap between innovation and everyday life. We aim to: Demystify technology by breaking down complex concepts into engaging, accessible conversations. Inspire curiosity about the future of science, digital culture, and human-machine interaction.

Explore impact by highlighting how technology shapes society, identity, and the way we live and work. Connect people through thoughtful storytelling that invites listeners from all backgrounds to imagine what’s next.
